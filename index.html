About me

01

I am a master’s student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology, where I began my postgraduate studies in September 2025. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Soran University.

My research focuses on natural language processing for Kurdish Sorani, with an interest in hate speech detection and language resources. Through SoraniX, I work on supporting research for a language with limited annotated data.

Beyond research, my background brings together teaching, design, event organization, and environmental advocacy. I am involved in community initiatives in the Kurdistan Region, including the 1000 Trees project in Soran.
